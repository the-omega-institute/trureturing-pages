02 / TRIVIAL OR NONTRIVIAL?
≡

Bind-only reuse

Instantiation, projection, renaming or packaging of existing results.

+

A substantive contribution

A construction, estimate or proposition that does real work in the proof.

✓

Formal verification checks that the argument holds.

FORMAL FOUNDATIONSEscape-rate definitions ↗Contribution rules ↗
The illustration fixes four states, a target and ordered-pair normalization. Relabeling preserves 4/12; separating one previously merged pair gives 2/12. These are illustrative exact counts, not measured rates for the collaboration cases. Escape gain and proof nontriviality have distinct definitions. Bind-only is judged after local expansion relative to a fixed upstream base; a substantive witness must be on the live proof path. A registered named open-problem resolution has its own admission basis.
