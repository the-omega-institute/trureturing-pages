Formal escape definition ↗
Information escape is intrinsic to a fixed state space X and selected catalog S: E_S = {(x,y): x ≠ y and every c_i in S gives equal readings on x and y}. The catalog concepts are derived from registered mathematical primitive bundles, not arbitrary labels attached to proofs. No external question is needed: the reference is state identity. The example fixes X to exactly two graphs and S to their sorted degree sequence. The graphs differ but both readings are (2,2,2,2,2,2), so both ordered distinct pairs escape. Connectedness makes their difference visible to the viewer; it is not an additional selected readout. Escape means the difference remains unrepresented by this catalog. If every available definition merges a pair, combinations of those definitions cannot separate it: extend the representation. The graph example illustrates the definition; it is not a new research claim.

04 / REMOVE IT: DOES THE DIFFERENCE ESCAPE AGAIN?

Remove triangle count: the two graphs become indistinguishable again. This isolates what that concept contributes beyond the rest.

δᵢ = ε(S ∖ {i}) − ε(S)

Contribution gain = escape without it − escape with it. Here: 1 − 0 = 1.
FORMAL FOUNDATIONSEscape rate & contribution gain ↗Distinction & escape gain ↗Blind-language obstruction ↗
For a fixed finite arena X with at least two states, ε(S) = |E_S| / (|X|(|X|−1)): the fraction of ordered different pairs still indistinguishable under all selected concepts. This two-graph example gives 2/2 with degree sequence alone, still 2/2 after relabeling, and 0/2 after adding triangle count. The full illustrated catalog contains degree sequence and triangle count. Removing triangle count raises escape from 0 to 1, giving its leave-one-out gain of 1. Removing degree sequence while retaining triangle count gives gain 0. Gain is relative to the same current catalog, not a comparison between historical submissions. The formal finite-catalog criterion equates positive gain with unique capture, strict kernel refinement and nonrecoverability from the remaining catalog. The example does not itself certify a novel theorem. Bind-only separately checks proof shape and substantive content on the live proof path. State space and denominator stay fixed; deleting difficult states would change the evaluation rather than demonstrate capture.

Read the harness rules ↗
These requirements work together. Retrieval establishes what is already available; exact matches are imported and applied directly. Literature checks verify the original statement, assumptions and solved scope. Intrinsic escape counts distinct states merged by every concept in the selected catalog; leave-one-out gain tests what a contribution uniquely captures. Bind-only review expands local aliases and helpers against a fixed upstream base and requires substantive content on the live proof path. Lean checks the formal claim and proof. A preregistered named external open-problem resolution has its own admission basis, even when the proof is a direct application of existing mathematics.
