↻

Read the harness rules ↗
These requirements work together. Retrieval establishes what is already available; exact matches are imported and applied directly. Literature checks verify the original statement, assumptions and solved scope. Information escape is defined relative to a representation and target. Bind-only review expands local aliases and helpers against a fixed upstream base and requires substantive content on the live proof path. Lean checks the formal claim and proof. A preregistered named external open-problem resolution has its own admission basis, even when the proof is a direct application of existing mathematics.
