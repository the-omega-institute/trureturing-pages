THE FILTERTRURETURING

No new distinction,
no new theorem.

✕

Wrappers

Restating, renaming or repackaging what is already proved.

✓

Real content

A step no existing result can supply: a new object, construction or estimate.

We call wrappers bind-only and reject them. A new theorem must carry an escape witness: a new fact on its live proof path. The expected new content is registered before the attempt.
