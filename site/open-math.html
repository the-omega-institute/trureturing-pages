06 / THE VERIFICATION HARNESSTRURETURING

Open participation. Explicit checks.

The harness combines formal verification with review of the intended mathematics.

✓

Machine checks

  • Lean checks formal proofs and their assumptions.
  • Admission checks reject sorry and unapproved axioms.
  • Frozen statement records detect changes.
↗

Independent review

  • Does the statement match its source?
  • Does the work add useful mathematical content?
  • Are reuse, attribution and limits clear?

Kernel verification establishes a formal statement under its assumptions. Review assesses meaning and value, and can make mistakes.

Explain that correctness, source fidelity and mathematical value are distinct. The bind-only policy has an explicit external open-problem exception. The information-escape audit currently reports warnings and is not a general blocking guarantee of non-triviality.

APPENDIX / SCOPE AND RIGHTSTRURETURING

Clear boundaries make collaboration easier.

Formal results

A checked proof establishes its exact formal statement under its assumptions. Source fidelity and significance still require review.

Dependencies and credit

The current Pages graph records module imports and separately labeled document or affinity relations. Git and PRs supply contribution records.

Reuse and useful novelty

Existing results receive credit. The contribution rules discourage wrappers and include an explicit exception for named external open-problem resolutions.

Licensing and consent

Repository-produced Lean code: Apache-2.0. Text: CC BY 4.0. Data: CC0. Third-party terms remain applicable. Any pilot training use needs explicit consent and agreed terms.
