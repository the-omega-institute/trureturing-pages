03 / WHAT TRURETURING IS

Not a problem set. A method for finding truth together.

People bring questions, ideas and sources. With an AI agent they turn them into Lean statements and proofs. The kernel decides what is true, and the result becomes a permanent node that others can build on.

IDEAS IN

TRUTH OUT

A node, or an honest open

A kernel-checked result in the shared graph, or a precisely stated question that remains open.

We are looking for the primes of truth: irreducible verified statements from which later results are built.
Stress that the product is the method, not a leaderboard of solved problems. Problems are how the method is exercised; the durable output is the graph and the people who learned to extend it.

06 / THE HARNESS

Strict rules, open door

1

Only the kernel decides truth

Lean 4 with Mathlib. No sorry, no unapproved axioms. Comments and prose carry no weight.

2

The same gates for everyone

No trusted authors. Admission depends on the change, never on who made it.

3

Truth only accumulates

A verified node is frozen. New rules may reject new bad work, but never overturn accepted truth.

4

Honest status

Every claim is proved, refuted, open, or explicitly assumed-unverified. Nothing is claimed that is not on record.

5

People choose direction, machines judge correctness

No gate waits for a person to decide right or wrong. People decide what is worth pursuing.

The rules are public and versioned in the repository. Changing a rule is itself a pull request through the same gates.

Strictness is what makes openness safe. Because the kernel and the gates do the judging, we can accept work from anyone, including models, without trusting them.

07 / NON-TRIVIAL BY DESIGN

Volume cannot counterfeit progress

Wrappers do not count

A new theorem that only instantiates, projects or repackages existing results is “bind-only”. Reviewers reject it; the credit stays with the result that already exists.

Declare the new content first

Before a probe, the contributor registers the non-trivial fact they expect and the criteria for success and refutation. No relabeling after the fact.

Say where the idea escapes

Each new theorem records where the difficulty was, how it was handled, what new information comes out, and where the problem continues.

SETTLED BY CITATION

A statement that a general theorem holds at n = 60 is an instantiation. New claims of that shape are closed by citing the general theorem, not by adding a theorem.

NEW CONTENT

Answering Problem 15.5 needed a new object, a specific five-element semigroup, that no existing result provides.

We measure progress by questions resolved and known ranges extended, not by counts of theorems, modules or lines.

Kernel checks and frozen truth are enforced by machine. Non-triviality and faithfulness to the source are enforced by independent review; the escape record is currently audited as a warning, not a blocking gate.

This is the part most relevant to training data quality: the harness is built to refuse cheap outputs that look like progress.

09 / LEAVE A TRACE

Every contribution stays visible, and so does what depends on it

  • Content-addressedEach frozen node is pinned by the hash of its statement. Change the statement and the pin no longer matches, so the change cannot pass unnoticed.
  • Real dependenciesEdges come from the proofs themselves, not from prose. Reuse shows up in the graph.
  • Public attributionPull-request records name the author, the agent and skill used, and the independent reviewers.
  • Public viewsThe Atlas shows concept families; Evolution shows how the graph grew release by release; Research lists resolved questions with sources.
This is the answer to how credit works in AI-assisted mathematics: the graph itself shows who contributed which node and how much later work rests on it.

10 / LEARNING BY DOING

A method you learn by using it

  1. Predict before you look.Register success and refutation criteria before the attempt.
  2. Search before you prove.Reuse exact matches from the repository, Mathlib and the literature.
  3. Separate what you know.Proved, refuted, open and assumed-unverified are different states.
  4. Measure instead of guessing.A reading and its source, never “probably”.
  5. When progress stalls, change the frame.A new representation, tool or level, not more grinding.
  6. Keep the result, not the diary.Failures become reusable criteria and regression tests.

WHAT A CONTRIBUTOR TAKES AWAY

How to state a claim exactly.

How to find what is already known.

How a proof actually fails, and what the failure teaches.

How to hand a result to others so they can build on it.

The agent explains each step; the machine verdict is immediate and impartial. A student and a professor get the same honest feedback.

These habits are written into the repository rules and skills, so every agent session practises them. That makes the harness a teaching environment as much as a library.

12 / WHY THIS MATTERS FOR OPEN MODELS

What the harness can offer an open math model

Verifiable signal

Every admitted result is kernel-checked and every rejection has a named reason: feedback that cannot be argued with.

Uncontaminated evaluation

Pre-registered open questions whose answers were unknown when they entered. Graded by the kernel, not by a reference answer.

Honest negative data

Refutations and countermodels are first-class results, and rejected pull requests stay public with the reasons they failed.

A place to put models to work

A model-agnostic harness: the same pull-request door for open-weight models, commercial agents and people.

reliable assistance, verifiable results, and the cost of sustained use

The announcement’s three measures. For any model working in the harness, we could report all three: the share of its changes that are admitted, what the kernel verifies, and the compute spent per admitted result.

14 / HONEST BOUNDARIES

What we guarantee, and what we do not claim

Guaranteed by machine

  • Lean kernel verification, with no sorry and only approved axioms.
  • Frozen results never silently change.
  • Required checks run on every pull request.

Guaranteed by independent review

  • A formal statement faithfully captures its source.
  • A new result is genuinely non-trivial.
  • Computational work answers a real question.

Not claimed

  • Theory prose is reference input, not truth.
  • Kernel-checked does not mean faithful to the source.
  • Team-reported competition results are not official rankings.
  • An open question stays marked open.
