Trureturing Upload

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026. This policy describes the local Trureturing Upload tool operated for the Omega Institute YouTube channel. Contact: omegadesci@gmail.com.

Google and YouTube access

With the channel owner's consent, the tool accesses the authorized channel's identifier and name, its uploaded-video list, and the titles, identifiers, visibility and processing status of its videos. It uses this information to confirm the destination channel, avoid duplicate uploads, and verify publication.

The tool uses the authorized YouTube permission to upload selected films, set their thumbnails, and change their visibility. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or billing. Google handles account login and consent; the tool does not receive or store the Google password.

Local storage and retention

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in local files restricted to the operating-system user. Upload session state and publication records are stored locally to resume interrupted transfers and avoid uploading the same film again. Publication records include the channel ID, video ID, title, visibility, release identifier and verification time. Temporary film downloads are removed after publication has been verified.

Local authorization is retained while the operator uses the tool. Publication records are retained for synchronization history until the operator removes them. Tokens and upload-session credentials are not included in public source repositories.

Use and disclosure

Google and YouTube data is used only to operate and verify the channel publishing workflow. The tool does not sell that data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails selected for publication are sent to YouTube and become available according to the visibility set by the channel owner. The underlying films are downloaded from their public release source.

Trureturing Upload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

The channel owner can revoke access through Google Account connections, stop the local synchronization task, and delete its local credentials and records. Deleting local records does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; those videos can be managed in YouTube Studio. Contact the operator at the address above for questions about local retention or deletion.

This website

These application information pages do not contain analytics scripts or collect login details. The website hosting provider may process ordinary HTTP request data under its own privacy policy.
